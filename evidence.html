TactiCall: evidence of prototype and progress

TactiCall is a home alerting system for deaf and hard of hearing (D/HH) residents. A hub learns the sounds a home already has, such as the fire alarm, doorbell and washing machine, and alerts the resident through vibration and an icon on a wearable.

This page supports our application to the EIT Culture & Creativity Spark Incubation Programme 2026 (priority area: Architecture). Applicant: Haofei Niu.

Prototype

The current prototype is a dedicated home hub and a wearable. It runs end to end on off-the-shelf modules, recognises sounds on the device and works without an internet connection. Residents can add their own sounds. An earlier version (2025) used a phone app with a haptic wristband.
