TactiCall is an assistive wearable system for deaf and hard-of-hearing people. A home hub listens and recognises sounds, microphones extend coverage to every room, and a wristband alerts you wherever you are.

the wristband

Every sound gets its own message.

Knocking, the doorbell, someone calling your name. Wherever you are, the wristband tells you which, in words you can read and a pattern you can feel.

day and night

Even when you’re asleep.

Hearing aids out, lights off. When the fire alarm goes off, the wristband lights up and vibrates to make sure you wake up. No more wearing hearing aids all day.

how it works

Sound in, touch out.

  1. 01

    It listens

    The home hub listens, and microphones extend coverage to every room.

  2. 02

    It understands

    An AI model trained for your home recognises what it heard: the doorbell, an alarm, your name. Every sound is processed locally.

Sounds everyone shares

doorbell / fire alarm / phone ringing / oven timer

Classic notification sounds are similar all over the world, so TactiCall recognises them straight away.

Sounds that are yours

your name / a knock on your door / a running tap / your cat

Record a few clips, like your flatmate calling your name, and within minutes TactiCall learns them.

Every household gets its own AI model, so TactiCall works with what’s already in your home. No rewiring, no transmitters.

the research

We tested it before we talked about it.

We’ve tested both how accurately TactiCall recognises sounds and how well people recognise its alerts, with very promising results. The full study is published by Springer.

TactiCall grew out of research at University of the Arts London, peer-reviewed and presented at the International Conference on Social Robotics + AI, 2025.

Niu, H., Akhond, S., & Samani, H. “TactiCall: ML-Powered Haptic Wristband for Alerting Hearing-Impaired Users.” Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNAI), Springer Nature, ICSR+AI 2025.

good questions

Before you ask

Do I need an Apple Watch or a special device?

No Apple Watch needed. TactiCall is its own system: a home hub that listens and recognises sounds, microphones that extend coverage to every room, and a wristband that alerts you wherever you are.

What will it cost?

We’re aiming for roughly £80 one-off for the dedicated wristband, plus £3–5/month for the app and its ongoing sound recognition. Final pricing isn’t locked in yet; the waitlist is the best way to hear first.

Is this a medical or assistive device I have to register?

No. Think of it more like AirPods than a hearing aid: a piece of consumer electronics anyone can wear, with no paperwork attached.

Does it listen to everything I say?

No. It listens for sound events, like a doorbell, an alarm or your name being called, not conversation. All sounds are processed locally; audio isn’t sent off for processing.

When can I actually get one?

We have working prototypes now, and we’re building our first home-trial version this December. Join the list and we’ll only email you when there’s real news.
